How is this calculated?

Each claim is scored on the strength of its evidence chain across 5 dimensions, then blended with the claim's own stated confidence:

  • 30%Evidence Coverage — expected upstream node types & declared evidence are present
  • 20%Statistical Validity — confidence value, result metrics & stats
  • 20%Reproducibility — runs carry git commit, environment, duration
  • 15%Data Provenance — artifacts have a tracked path, version & type
  • 15%Pipeline Integrity — the chain motivation → result is complete

Final score blends 40% structure + 60% the claim's confidence (claims below 0.5 confidence are penalized harder; no stated confidence caps the score). Thresholds: green ≥ 0.72, yellow ≥ 0.4, red < 0.4. Toggle Credibility in the top bar to colour claim nodes by score.
